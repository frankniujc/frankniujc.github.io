## TL;DR

**Existing steering methods remain unreliable once control objectives look like real deployment.** RealControlBench moves steering evaluation beyond isolated, single-attribute generations to execution-grounded, multi-turn agent interactions across five dimensions: instruction-following control, agentic tool-use decision control, reasoning-performance steering, reasoning-length control, and trajectory-conditioned safety awareness. It measures both target controllability and capability preservation, and finds that current methods often substantially degrade task performance.
