## TL;DR

**Supervised Multi-Dimensional Scaling (SMDS) is a model-agnostic method to automatically discover feature manifolds in language models**, where prior efforts targeted specific geometries for specific features and thus lacked generalization. Applying SMDS to temporal reasoning as a case study, we find features forming circles, lines, and clusters whose geometry consistently reflects the properties of the concepts they represent, stays stable across model families and sizes, actively supports reasoning, and dynamically reshapes in response to context changes — supporting a model of entity-based reasoning in which LMs encode and transform structured representations.
